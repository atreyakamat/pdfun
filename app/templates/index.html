    How the Zero Storage Limit Works

    Unlike traditional web tools that impose 10MB, 25MB, or 50MB file size limits, pdfun uses streaming asynchronous chunk pipelines. When you upload a large PDF (even several gigabytes in size), it is streamed directly to isolated disk storage in 1MB chunks without ballooning the server's RAM.

    Privacy & Automatic Cleanup

    Files are stored only for the duration of your session. An automated background sweeper periodically deletes temporary workspaces after 2 hours of inactivity. You can also click the "Delete Session" button at any time to immediately wipe all uploaded and generated files.
